# Recording the demo video

Target: **90 seconds**. Longer than that and clients stop watching.

Record on your **phone**, not the laptop — the laptop microphone doesn't work, and the
phone's screen recorder captures screen and voice together in one step.

---

## 1. Set the phone up

**Android:** swipe down twice → **Screen Record** → turn **Microphone ON** before starting.
**iPhone:** Control Centre → long-press the **Screen Recording** button → **Microphone ON**.

Then:

- Silent mode ON, notifications off (a WhatsApp banner mid-recording looks unprofessional)
- Open `https://vapi-calling-agent-demo.vercel.app` in Chrome
- Full screen — hide the browser address bar by scrolling down once
- Wired earphones in, so the agent's voice doesn't bleed into your microphone

## 2. Rehearse once without recording

Run the whole call once. Know where Aria pauses. The second take is always better.

---

## 3. The shot list

| Time | On screen | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | The page, sitting idle | Don't touch anything. Let them see the branding |
| 0:05–0:10 | Tap **Talk to Aria** | Let the orb start pulsing |
| 0:10–0:20 | Aria greets you | Stay silent. Her greeting is the hook |
| 0:20–0:55 | The booking conversation | Run the script below |
| 0:55–1:10 | Appointment card filling | **Slow down here.** Let each field land |
| 1:10–1:20 | Green **Confirmed** card | Hold the shot for a full 3 seconds |
| 1:20–1:30 | Scroll up to the transcript | Show the whole conversation written out |

### The script

> "Hi, I'd like to book a cleaning."
> "No, first time."
> "Sarah Chen."
> "512-555-0188."
> "Actually — do you take Delta Dental?"
> "Great, let's do the cleaning then."
> "Thursday works."
> "Yes, that's right."

Speak slowly and clearly. Wait for Aria to finish each question — overlapping speech is
the fastest way to make a demo look broken.

---

## 4. The voiceover

Two options. **Option B is stronger for a Pakistani client.**

**Option A — no voiceover.** Send it raw. The conversation speaks for itself. Add text
captions in editing instead.

**Option B — narrate in Urdu over the English call.** Record the screen first, then add
your voice over it in editing. This works well: the client hears a professional English
agent *and* your explanation in their own language.

Suggested narration, timed to the shot list:

| At | Say |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Ye aap ke clinic ka front desk hai. Jab koi mareez call karta hai, ye phone uthata hai." |
| 0:20 | "Main yahan mareez ka role kar rahi hoon." |
| 0:40 | "Dekhein — jo tafseelat wo deti ja rahi hai, dayein taraf khud likhi ja rahi hain." |
| 0:55 | "Beech mein insurance ka sawal poocha — jawab de kar wapas booking par aa gayi. Apni jagah nahi bhooli." |
| 1:10 | "Appointment confirm. Poora call ninety seconds, kharcha chhe cents." |
| 1:25 | "Ye raat do baje bhi isi tarah kaam karta hai, chhutti wale din bhi." |

---

## 5. Editing

**CapCut** (free, works on the phone) is enough:

1. Trim the dead air at both ends
2. Cut any pause longer than 2 seconds
3. Add a title card at the start: **"AI Front Desk — Live Demo"** (3 seconds)
4. Add an end card: your name, and one line — *"Answers every call. Books every appointment."*
5. Captions on the key moments, especially where the card fills in
6. Export at 1080p

Do **not** add background music under the call audio. It makes the agent's voice harder
to judge, and judging the voice is the whole point.

---

## 6. If you want the raw call audio

Vapi records every call. To get a clean audio file with no room noise:

**Vapi dashboard → Calls → click the call → play or download the recording.**

Useful if you want a polished audio track underneath a screen recording made silently.

---

## 7. What to send the client

1. The 90-second video
2. The live link, so they can try it themselves: `https://vapi-calling-agent-demo.vercel.app`
3. One line of text: *"Try it yourself — click Talk to Aria and ask to book a cleaning."*

Letting them try it on their own phone is more persuasive than any video. The video is
what gets forwarded to the people who weren't in the meeting.
